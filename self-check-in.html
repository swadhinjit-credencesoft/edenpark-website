Seamless arrival. Your schedule, your pace.

Arriving late after a concert, checking in between meetings, or beating the event crowds? Our contactless check-in gets you into your room without queues.

How it works

The three-step process

1

Receive your code

On the morning of your arrival we send a secure check-in email and SMS to the contact details on your reservation, containing your code and room allocation.

2

Park with ease

Drive straight into the main entrance at 36 Sandringham Road and park in any designated guest space. No permits or dashboard tickets required.

3

Collect your key

Use your code to open the key box and collect your room key. This is a self-service motel — there are no card-based door locks. Please return the key to the same box when you leave.

Proactive peace of mind

Secure, reliable and monitored

Our renovated 1910 villa has enhanced perimeter lighting and active security measures. While check-in is contactless, on-site management and support are always available — call 0800 AT EDEN if you need a hand.

Before you arrive

Pre-arrival checklist

  • Complete any pre-arrival registration forms sent by email.
  • Save your check-in code on your mobile device.
  • Enter via the main driveway at 36 Sandringham Road.
  • Return your key to the key box when you check out.

Ready for your stay?
